# Story prologue

Five animated scenes introduce the warm city, the Heart that burns memories, five seals, the last keeper and the descent. English is the default; Russian remains an optional translation. Next, Back, scene selection, sound, pause, automatic advance, motion and skip work through the mouse interface. Keyboard controls remain available.

The prologue uses its own clock outside the campaign simulation. Hidden tabs and pause stop its reading clock. The final scene waits for an explicit departure. A pointer-events rule on the prologue root fixes the inherited mouse-input block found during the audit.

The story uses captions and synthesized music, without a recorded voice actor. Tests cover timeline boundaries, bilingual scene content, pause and completion behavior; browser checks verify actual pointer interaction.
