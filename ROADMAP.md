# Future development

Current submission fixes are complete in version 2.3.2. These items are future proposals, not implemented features:

1. Memory choices with different endings and clearer consequences.
2. Mini-bosses and more enemy coordination with readable warnings.
3. Water, steam and ash interactions, with repeatable environment tests.
4. Exact battle saves and replay tools before daily seeded challenges.
5. Controller and touch controls, broader accessibility checks and long-form balance testing.

Preserve the separation of simulation and rendering. Add one feature at a time and report its verified behavior and limits. Human five-floor playtesting remains valuable even after the automated campaign experiment passes.
